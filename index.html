Why I Want to Study This Course

A stronger frontend foundation—and a community to grow with.

I want to study IT – Tech for women because I want to strengthen my skills as a web developer in a supportive environment with other women who are building careers in tech.

I already have some basic knowledge of frontend development. What I want to add is a stronger frontend foundation: modern frameworks, design libraries and Figma, so that I can build interfaces that are not only working but also clear and pleasant for the people who use them. I also want to learn how developers and designers work together.

This course will also gives me something I cannot learn alone: a network. Women are still a minority in IT, and meeting women who are starting out and women who are already established gives me role models, advice and confidence. As a woman who came to Sweden from Cameroon and is building a new career in IT, that community matters a lot to me.

Finally, I want to turn my training into a job. The course is practical and connected to the industry, and it points towards internships and work. I want to leave with a stronger portfolio, a certificate and contacts that help me get my first role as a web developer.

Why I Will Be a Good Future Developer

Keep learning. Solve problems. Build for people.

I will be a good future developer because I keep learning, I finish what I start and I understand the people I build for.

Before my studies I worked as an IT instructor and support technician, and later in administration and customer service. This means I can explain technical ideas clearly, listen to users and keep my work organised and accurate. I also volunteer with the Swedish Red Cross, where I help customers, which reminds me that software is made for real people.

When I am stuck I troubleshoot step by step, look for answers and ask for help. I am reliable, careful and curious, and I want to grow into a developer who builds solutions that are stable, useful and easy to use.

Personal Letter

A little about my journey, motivation and goals.

Dear Course Coordinator and Admissions Team at Sundsgårdens folkhögskola,

My name is Glory Ngum Foncha and I am applying for the course IT – Tech for women. I would like to tell you who I am and why this course is the right next step for me.

My story with technology started a long time ago. In Cameroon I co-founded CNET_SIRD, where I worked as an IT instructor and support technician. I taught computer skills to students and staff, installed and maintained IT systems and networks, and helped users solve problems. I enjoyed helping people understand technology and use it with confidence. Later I worked as an administrative assistant, which taught me accuracy, reliability and good customer service.

After moving to Sweden I decided to return to IT and become a developer. I completed a fullstack development bootcamp at ReDI School of Digital Integration, I have worked with HTML, CSS, Git and GitHub.

I want to take this course because I want to become a stronger web developer, and I know where my gaps are. I want to learn Figma and design, modern design libraries and frameworks, and how to make interfaces that people enjoy using. I also want to build a portfolio that shows both my technical work and my sense of design.

Just as important is the community. Women are still a minority in IT, and I know how much it helps to meet other women who are also learning to code, and women who have already built a career. I want to give and receive support, learn from guest speakers, and be part of a group where it feels natural to ask questions.

I am ready to study full-time and to work hard. I am used to structured study, and I know how to keep going when something is difficult. I also like to help others, so I will gladly share what I know with my classmates.

My goal is to work as a web developer in Sweden, and to build products that are reliable and kind to their users. This course would give me the design skills, the confidence and the network to get there. Thank you for reading my application. I would be very happy to join the course and to contribute to the group.

Kind regards,
